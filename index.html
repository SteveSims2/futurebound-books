The story

A future in flux.
A connection that changes everything.

Evan Hale lives in a future where institutions measure people's worth by their usefulness. After a corporate failure, he leaves the life he knew behind and steps into an uncertain world.

There he meets Mara, a refugee with a mysterious past and unusual abilities that challenge familiar ideas about human identity. As technological change and social instability reshape the world around them, Evan and Mara's relationship begins to grow.

Meridian is a story of autonomy and belonging—and of the questions that emerge when the future changes what it means to be human.

The author

Steven Earl Sims
